About Me

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

For 4 years at QualityKiosk Technologies, banks relied on me to find what would break before their customers did. I tested 12 modules across 3 banks: Commercial Bank of Dubai's new corporate internet banking platform through to its first production release, Kotak Mahindra Bank's mobile account-opening apps, and India's National Pension System for NSDL. I then completed an MSc in Software Engineering at the University of Hertfordshire, winning the University Prize for the highest CGPA on the programme.
